About Me

I am an electrical engineering student at the Georgia Institute of Technology, currently interning on the Starship RF team at SpaceX in Hawthorne, California. My work is in avionics: flight computers, RF telemetry, and the data-acquisition electronics that support launch vehicles and spacecraft.

At Georgia Tech I have spent three years with the Yellow Jacket Space Program. As Director of Avionics I led the team behind the Vespula and Felicette liquid rockets, which reached 56,000 ft and 47,000 ft, the two highest flights by collegiate liquid rockets. I now advise the program. I have also interned at Vast on Haven-1, at SpaceX Starbase on Starship ground systems, and at Honeybee Robotics on NASA's Dragonfly mission.
